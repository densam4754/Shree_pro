# Onboarding Images

Please download and add the following images to this directory:

1. **fuel_station.jpg** - Image of a fuel station/gas station
   - Search: "fuel station" or "gas station" on Pixabay
   - Should represent fuel management

2. **sales_tracking.jpg** - Image related to sales/transactions
   - Search: "sales" or "business transaction" on Pixabay
   - Should represent tracking and monitoring

3. **customers.jpg** - Image related to customers/people/business
   - Search: "customers" or "business people" on Pixabay
   - Should represent customer management

4. **analytics.jpg** - Image related to analytics/data/reports
   - Search: "analytics" or "data visualization" on Pixabay
   - Should represent reports and analytics

## Image Requirements:
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Recommended size: 1080x1920 (portrait) or 1920x1080 (landscape)
- High quality images that work well with white text overlay
- Images should be relevant to fuel station management

## Download from:
https://pixabay.com/images/search/

## Note:
The app will use gradient fallbacks if images are not found, but background images provide a better user experience.
